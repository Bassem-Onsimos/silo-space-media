Spotlighting custom design

Silo Space Media partnered with Cabinets by Design to bring their custom cabinetry to life through video, photography, and social content. From behind-the-scenes craftsmanship to finished kitchen transformations, we captured the detail and quality that sets their work apart.

The result is a content library built to showcase Cabinets by Design across social platforms, their website, and future marketing campaigns, helping potential clients see not just the product, but the craft behind it.

Film · 16:9

High quality video

High quality video does what photos alone cannot: it captures detail, craftsmanship, and story in a way that builds trust and drives engagement. On social media, video gets shared more and seen more. On a website, it keeps visitors engaged longer and builds confidence before they ever walk through the door.

For craftsmanship-driven businesses like custom cabinetry, video turns a finished product into a story worth sharing, and a customer worth converting.
